Still missing

What is left, in the order it matters

Ranked by how much each one changes what you can trust, not by how hard it is.

01

Calibration backtest

Until this runs, the ranking is internally consistent and externally unproven. Labelled historical launches are public, so this is the cheapest credibility available and the single biggest change to what the output is worth.

a weekend
02

Archive and variation operators

Right now each cycle starts from scratch. An archive that keeps one elite per cell, plus mutation and crossover on survivors, is what turns a one-shot generator into a search that compounds across nights.

a day
03

Scheduled harvesters

The evidence was gathered by hand this time. Until the harvesters run on their own, the system cannot notice a capability crossing a threshold while you sleep, which is the whole timing edge.

a day
04

Sneaky advocate

An agent whose job is the most persuasive possible pitch for a deliberately bad idea. Whatever it sneaks past the panel becomes a hard negative. This is the defence against the ranker being gamed by good prose, and nothing else covers it.

half a day
05

Probe execution and feedback

The system designs probes but nothing closes the loop. Until a real ad test comes back and reweights the scorer, every number above the probe line is a proxy.

needs your budget
06

Normalised audience taxonomy

Coverage is currently computed over raw audience strings, which inflates the void share to a meaningless 98%. The siRNA version of this number is meaningful because its axes are a closed vocabulary.

half a day
